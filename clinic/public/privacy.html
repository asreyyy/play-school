Privacy Policy

Last updated: [date]

The clinic ("we", "us") respects your privacy. Mental-health care depends on trust, and this page explains what personal information we collect through this website and how we protect it.

What we collect

  • When you book online: your name, phone number, email (optional), your chosen doctor, service, date and time, and anything you choose to write in the booking form.
  • During your care: your doctor may keep clinical notes about your sessions, as required for safe treatment.

Why we use it

  • To schedule, confirm, reschedule or cancel your appointments and contact you about them.
  • To provide and record your medical care.
  • For billing and to meet our legal and professional record-keeping duties.

We do not sell your information or use it for advertising.

Who can see it

  • Clinical notes can be read only by the doctor who wrote them and the clinic administrator.
  • Reception staff can see your contact details and appointments, but not clinical notes.
  • We share information outside the clinic only with your consent, or where the law requires it (for example, to prevent serious harm).
  • Our website is hosted by [hosting provider, e.g. Render], which stores the data securely on our behalf.

How we protect it

The website uses an encrypted (HTTPS) connection, staff accounts are password-protected, and access is limited by role. Data is backed up regularly.
